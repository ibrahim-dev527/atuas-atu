About

About ATUAS

The home of Accountancy students at Accra Technical University (ATU), and the ATU chapter of the national ATUAS body.

ATUAS at ATU

Accountancy students of Accra Technical University, united.

ATUAS-ATU is the student association of the Accountancy Department at Accra Technical University. It brings HND, BTech and Diploma-to-BTech students together under one roof, and speaks for them on campus and beyond.

Led by an elected executive, the association runs academic support, career events, quiz and competition teams, and the traditions that make department life memorable, all under the motto Commitment Is Vital.

At a glance

Home institution: Accra Technical University
Motto: Commitment Is Vital
Who we serve: every Accountancy student in the department
Affiliation: national ATUAS body (ATUAS-GH)

The Wider Family

ATU is part of a national body.

The Association of Technical University Accountancy Students (ATUAS) is a professional and academic student organization operating across technical universities in Ghana, representing students pursuing HND and degree programmes in Accountancy.

ATUAS bridges classroom theory and industry practice through seminars, career guidance workshops and educational tours, run in partnership with professional bodies including the Institute of Chartered Accountants, Ghana (ICAG) and the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA). It serves as a platform for networking, peer support and academic advocacy for future finance and audit professionals.

Chapters & leadership

ATUAS operates through autonomous local chapters at member institutions, including Accra Technical University (ATU) and Koforidua Technical University (KTU), aligned with each institution's faculty.

Each chapter is run by elected student executives, presidents, financial officers, public relations officers and others, who organize annual hand-over ceremonies, general assemblies and student welfare programmes.

The ATU Chapter

Mission, vision & values

Mission

To unite and serve every Accountancy student at Accra Technical University by promoting academic excellence, professional growth and student welfare, and by representing the department with integrity within the SRC, ATUAS-GH and the wider academic community.

Vision

To be the leading Accountancy students' association among Ghana's technical universities, producing disciplined, ethical and industry-ready accountants who carry the name of ATU with pride.

Motto

"Commitment Is Vital", guiding every programme, event and decision the association makes.

Aims & Objectives

What the Local Chapter aims to do.

  1. Unify every student in the department, HND, BTech and Diploma students advancing to BTech alike.
  2. Create awareness of Accountancy programmes locally and internationally, as guided by ATUAS-GH and the SRC.
  3. Educate members on modern Accountancy practices and technologies through events and programmes run in partnership with ATUAS-GH and industry experts.
  4. Advocate for the academic and social welfare of all students.
  5. Serve as the department's official mouthpiece within the SRC, ATUAS National, and the broader academic community.
Alumni

Our luminaries.

Every great department is measured by the people it sends out into the world. The alumni of ATUAS are our luminaries: graduates who walked the same corridors, sat the same papers and led the same association, and who now shine in audit firms, banks, public institutions, businesses and further studies.

They matter to the department in three ways. They prove that an Accountancy education at ATU leads somewhere. They open doors, through internships, referrals and professional advice. And they set the standard that today's students and executives are proud to follow.

If you are an ATUAS alumnus or alumna, we want to hear from you. Mentor a student, speak at an event, or simply stay connected.
