Annual Tradition

ATUAS Week

A week-long celebration of Accountancy student life at ATU, academic, social and cultural activities held together under one programme. Full schedule and highlights are added closer to each edition.

Expect academic talks, sports, cultural nights and the community service that bind the department together. The programme is announced by the executives ahead of each edition.

Upcoming seminar dates and speakers will be listed here once confirmed.

Annual Tradition

Football Gala

The department's annual football gala. An archive of past editions with winning teams, photos and results will appear here once confirmed.

Friendly rivalry, school spirit and a day off from the books. Fixtures, teams and results are shared with the department as each gala approaches.
Annual Tradition

Dinner & Awards Night

A yearly evening recognizing outstanding members, executives and contributions to the department.

A night to dress up, celebrate achievements and say thank you to the members who made the year count.
