About ATUAS

One department, one voice, one commitment.

ATUAS is the local chapter uniting all Accountancy students at Accra Technical University, HND, Diploma and BTech students, including those advancing from Diploma or HND to BTech, under ATUAS-GH and the SRC.

The association creates awareness of Accountancy programmes locally and internationally, educates members on modern accounting practices and technologies through events run with ATUAS-GH and industry experts, advocates for members' academic and social welfare, and serves as the department's official voice within the SRC, ATUAS National, and the wider academic community.

TBSC Quiz Club

South-East Zonal Champions, 2026.

TBSC is the Tertiary Business Sense Challenge, and ATU has been in it from day one. In 2026 our quiz club did not just compete, it rewrote the record books.

1st technical university at the nationals 2 trophies in 2026 2026 South-East zonal champions
See quiz activities

TBSC simply means the Tertiary Business Sense Challenge. ATU joined from the very first edition and has stayed in the competition to this day. Over the years our teams have travelled to compete, learned a great deal and kept getting sharper. Last year was good. 2026 was exceptional.

This year we broke records and set new ones. ATU became the first technical university to advance to the nationals of the TBSC, the first university to defeat the University of Ghana in the South-East zone, and the first university other than the University of Ghana to win the South-East zonal title. That makes us the 2026 South-East zonal champions.

The story did not stop there. During their week celebration, the University of Ghana invited us to a quiz contest, together with the University of Professional Studies, Accra (UPSA). ATU won the event. We came home with two trophies: one from that week celebration and one as South-East zonal champions.

Where we are now: reigning zonal champions, carrying two trophies and a new standard for every ATU team that comes after us.
