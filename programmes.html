Why This Programme

Why study Accounting at ATU?

The BTech Accounting and Finance Analytics programme combines accounting, finance, data analytics and modern accounting technologies, pairing practical learning and computer applications with the judgement needed for business decision-making and professional growth.

Career pathways graduates can pursue

  • Junior Accountant
  • Audit Assistant
  • Tax Advisor
  • Forensic Accounting Assistant
  • Data Analyst
  • Risk and Compliance Officer
  • Investment Analyst
